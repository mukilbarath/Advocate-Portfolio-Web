Our Legal Process

01

Initial Consultation

Confidential meeting in chambers or via secure call to comprehend the facts, timeline, and relief sought.

02

Review of Documents

Exhaustive forensic and statutory scrutiny of title deeds, notices, chargesheets, or prior pleadings.
